Bible Animations

3D animated tours of places and events in Scripture.

Nehemiah 3

Nehemiah’s Wall

Fly around Jerusalem as its walls and gates are rebuilt, section by section, in the order Nehemiah records them.

Inspired by the original fly-by created by Luke Thomas.

 Nehemiah 12:27–43

The Dedication of the Wall

Walk the finished wall of Jerusalem with the two great choirs of thanksgiving, one each way round the city, until they meet in the house of God.

 Exodus 25–40

The Tabernacle

Watch the tent of meeting go up at Sinai, piece by piece, in the order Moses set it up in Exodus 40, from the silver bases to the cloud of glory.

 Exodus 28 · Leviticus 8:6–9

The Priestly Garments

Watch Moses wash Aaron and dress him as high priest, garment by garment, in the order of Leviticus 8, from the linen tunic to the gold plate on his turban.

 Leviticus 16

The Day of Atonement

Follow Aaron through the Day of Atonement, step by step through Leviticus 16: the linen garments, the lots for the two goats, the incense and the blood inside the veil, and the goat sent into the wilderness.

 1 Kings 6–7 · 2 Chronicles 3–4

Solomon’s Temple

Watch Solomon’s temple rise on Mount Moriah, part by part, in the order of 1 Kings 6–7: the stone house and its side chambers, the cedar and gold within, the cherubim, and Hiram’s bronze pillars and Sea.

 Ezekiel 40–43

Ezekiel’s Temple

Follow the man with the measuring rod as he shows Ezekiel the temple of his vision on a very high mountain: the gates and courts, the altar, the house and its side chambers, until the glory of the LORD returns by the east gate.

 Acts 13–14

Paul’s First Missionary Journey

Fly the route of Paul and Barnabas’s first journey over the real land and sea: from Antioch to Cyprus, over the Taurus Mountains to Pisidian Antioch, Iconium, Lystra and Derbe, and back, with a ship on the sea crossings.

 Acts 15:36–18:22

Paul’s Second Missionary Journey

Fly the route of Paul’s second journey over the real land and sea: through Galatia to Troas, across to Macedonia, to Philippi, Thessalonica and Berea, Athens and Corinth, and home by Ephesus and Jerusalem.

 Acts 18:23–21:17

Paul’s Third Missionary Journey

Fly the route of Paul’s third journey over the real land and sea: two years in Ephesus, Macedonia and Greece, then down the coast of Asia by Troas, Miletus and Tyre to Jerusalem with the collection for the poor.
